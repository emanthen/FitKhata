# Front Desk Quick Guide

One page. Keep this taped under the counter for your first week.

## Sign in

Go to your gym's FitKhata web address, enter your phone and password (or
tap **OTP** to sign in with a code texted to your phone instead).

## Check in a member

- **Normal case:** members check themselves in at the gate tablet by
  scanning their QR pass — you don't need to do anything.
- **Gate is down, or a member forgot their phone:** go to **Members**,
  search by name/phone/member code, open their profile, and use the
  manual check-in action. If the system shows a **red** result (expired,
  frozen, or owes money), you must type a reason before it lets you check
  them in anyway — this is logged.

## Register a new member

**Members → New member.** Phone number first — if they already have a
record (e.g. from a trial), it'll show up so you don't create a
duplicate. Fill in the rest, take their photo, and have them sign the
waiver on screen.

## Sell a membership / collect a payment

From a member's profile: **Sell** to start a new membership (pick a plan,
any add-ons, a promo code if they have one), or **Collect payment** on an
existing unpaid invoice. Choose how they're paying — cash, manual eSewa,
bank transfer, or cheque — and enter the amount. For cash, you'll need an
open cash shift first (see below).

## Cash shift

**Billing → Cash shift.** Open one at the start of your shift with the
starting float in the drawer. At the end of your shift, count every note
and coin into the denomination counter — the system tells you if it
doesn't match what it expects, and anything off by more than Rs. 100
notifies the owner automatically.

## Receipts

Every payment has a receipt. From the member's profile you can open the
PDF, text it to the member's phone, or — if this branch has the FitKhata
hardware bridge set up — print it straight to the thermal printer.

## If something's wrong

Use the feedback button (top right, speech-bubble icon) from anywhere in
the app to send a message straight to FitKhata support — it automatically
includes which page you were on.
